Lionel Andrés Messi was born on June 24, 1987, in Rosario, Argentina. He grew up in a tight-knit family that loved football, joining the local club Grandoli at age four and later playing for Newell's Old Boys. At age 11, doctors diagnosed him with a growth hormone deficiency. His local club could not pay for his medical care, but FC Barcelona offered to pay for his treatment if he moved to Spain. At age 13, Messi and his family moved to Barcelona, where he joined the famous La Masia youth academy. Messi quickly rose through the youth ranks and made his official first-team debut for FC Barcelona at age 17. During his 17 years with the club, he won 10 La Liga titles and four UEFA Champions League crowns, scoring a record 672 goals for the team. Known for his incredible dribbling, vision, and precise left foot, he earned a record eight Ballon d'Or awards as the world's best player. In 2021, he left Barcelona to play for Paris Saint-Germain (PSG), and in 2023, he joined Inter Miami in Major League Soccer. At the international level, Messi led Argentina to victory in the 2021 Copa América and the 2022 FIFA World Cup in Qatar, where he won his second Golden Ball award. With over 900 career goals, he remains widely regarded as one of the greatest football players in the history of the sport
